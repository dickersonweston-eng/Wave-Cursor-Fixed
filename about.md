# Wave Cursor Fixed

Replaces your OS cursor with your equipped wave icon, matching your actual colors, glow, and trail — everywhere except inside a level, where it's hidden to avoid interfering with gameplay.

This is a rebuilt version of the original Wave Cursor mod, fixed to avoid the performance issues the original had.

## Features
- Cursor follows your mouse as your wave icon
- Matches your equipped primary/secondary color and glow
- Optional trail effect using your equipped trail style
- Adjustable cursor size
- Automatically hidden while playing a level
